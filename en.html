01 / THE STORY

When opportunity comes calling

A room, a city, a choice.

Shanghai, 1992. News of securities moves through newspapers and telephone calls.Miss Su lives in a room where everyday life quietly shifts with the times.

Cut of Fortune 1984–1993 is a walk-in physical installation set against the early years of China’s stock market. Visitors enter Miss Su’s room, pick up the telephone, place a teacup in its marked spot, and finally choose at a subscription machine that prints a story ticket.

The installation uses share subscription certificates to connect a city’s memories with individual lives. A phone call, a television image triggered by the teacup, and a ticket in the visitor’s hand turn uncertainty into a physical experience.

03 / THE TELEVISION

One TV, two ways of seeing

The cup changes the channel.

As visitors enter, the television plays footage of the securities boom. Placing the teacup in its marked spot switches it to Miss Su’s life. One gesture connects the market’s noise with a person’s everyday world.

05 / BACKGROUND FILMS

The era beyond the room

An atmosphere, not a trigger.

Three vertical films build the atmosphere of the exhibition: subscription tickets and shifting market charts on either side, with archival views of the city and the securities boom in the centre.

All three are background displays. They are separate from the teacup-controlled television and do not change when the cup moves.

06 / OBJECTS & MAKING

From paper to place

The making

The work is made of objects visitors can hold, touch and take away. A foldout guides them through the room, while the machine and printed tickets carry the final part of the story.

A tangible ending

After a choice, the story comes out of the machine.

Each result ticket follows a fictional character’s life. Visitors take away the printed ticket and an uncertain ending.

07 / WEB DEMO

Try a small piece of the experience

A choice under uncertainty

This is a short web adaptation of the physical installation. The telephone, teacup placement, TV switch and printed ticket belong to the on-site experience. Choose a subscription ticket and step into the life of a person behind it. Your story is drawn at random; return, and you may live another.
